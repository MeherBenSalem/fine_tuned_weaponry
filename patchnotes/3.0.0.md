# Runeborn 3.0.0: Rise of the Runeborn

Fine Tuned Weaponry is reborn as **Runeborn**, a soulslike RPG progression mod. This is a new mod
(mod id `runeborn`), so items from Fine Tuned Weaponry 2.x do not carry over.

## Highlights
- **Runes and rune levels.** Every kill pays runes. Spend them at an Ember Shrine on eight attributes:
  Vigor, Mind, Endurance, Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Faith and Arcane. Die and your runes
  stay where you fell.
- **Better together with RPG Attribute System.** Shrine level-ups need RPG Attribute System
  installed, and every rune level you buy also grants RPG levels to spend in its Level Up screen.
- **74 armaments in 19 classes** with physical, magic, fire, lightning and holy damage, S–E scaling,
  requirements, status buildup, 23 weapon arts and 12 spells.
- **Reinforcement and affinities.** Up to +25 with tempering stones or +10 with lustrous stones, plus
  13 whetstone affinities.
- **Ember Shrines.** Kindle, rest, level up, reinforce, infuse, divide flasks, fast travel, trade,
  exchange relics, equip crown shards and seek rebirth.
- **Seven Sovereigns** summoned with sigils, plus relics and crown shards from the Wither, Ender
  Dragon, Warden and Elder Guardian. Champions with 10 affixes roam a world that grows deadlier with
  distance.
- **Clean UI.** Stat bars, a rune counter, flasks, target and boss bars, cinematic banners, a status
  screen and the **Codex**, an in-game guide with crafting recipes.
- **Feats.** A Runeborn advancement tab with 22 milestones.

## Controls
<kbd>R</kbd> weapon art · <kbd>Left Alt</kbd> dodge roll · <kbd>I</kbd> status and Codex.

## Downloads
Forge and Fabric for 1.20.1, NeoForge and Fabric for 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3. Fabric needs Fabric API.
