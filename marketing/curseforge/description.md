## Soulslike RPG progression for Minecraft

Slay foes for **runes**, rest at **Ember Shrines** to level up, and grow eight attributes that scale
**74 armaments**. Reinforce and infuse your weapon, fight leveled champions, and summon **seven
Sovereigns** for their relics and crown shards. Clean menus, a readable HUD and an in-game Codex
explain it all as you play.

**3.0 "Rise of the Runeborn"** is available for **Fabric and Forge on 1.20.1** and **Fabric and
NeoForge on 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3**. Pair it with **RPG Attribute System** for shared leveling.

![Runes & Levels](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-runes.png)

![Grow by runes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-levelup.png)

- Every kill pays **runes**: more for higher-level foes, far more for champions and Sovereigns.
- Spend them at an Ember Shrine on **Vigor, Mind, Endurance, Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Faith
  and Arcane**. Your rune level is the sum of your attributes, and each level costs more than the last.
- Every stat previews **current → after** before you confirm: HP, FP, stamina, poise, discovery,
  warding, healing and status potency, plus your weapon's attack power.
- Fall and your runes stay where you died, marked by a green glow. Walk back to reclaim them. Die on
  the way and they are gone.
- Begin as one of **10 origins**, from the balanced Knight-Errant to the Ashborn, who rises with nothing.

![Armaments](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-armaments.png)

![Seventy-four ways to fight](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-armaments.png)

- **74 armaments in 19 classes:** daggers, straight, thrusting and curved swords, katanas,
  twinblades, greatswords, colossal swords, axes, greataxes, hammers, great hammers, spears,
  halberds, reapers, fists, whips, staves and sacred seals.
- Physical, magic, fire, lightning and holy damage, with **S–E scaling grades** and attribute
  requirements. Wield a weapon you can't handle and its damage drops hard.
- Bleed, frost, poison and blight **build up** with every hit and burst when full.
- **23 weapon arts** on <kbd>R</kbd>, paid with FP: Unsheathe, Crimson Flurry, Stormcall, Lunar Wave,
  Shadowstep, Voidstar Fall and more.
- **12 spells.** Staves cast sorceries that scale with Intelligence; sacred seals cast incantations
  that scale with Faith.
- Vanilla and modded swords, axes and tridents scale with your attributes too.

![Reinforce and infuse](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-forge.png)

- **Tempering stones** take standard armaments to **+25**, and **lustrous stones** take legendary
  ones to **+10**. Foes drop them, with higher tiers deeper in the world.
- **13 affinities** (Heavy, Keen, Quality, Magic, Fire, Flame Art, Lightning, Sacred, Cold, Blood,
  Poison, Occult) reshape a weapon's damage and scaling. Craft a blank whetstone, then add reagents.
- Every change shows the attack power and scaling grades it gives before you pay.

![Ember Shrines](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-shrines.png)

![Rest at the shrine](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-shrine.png)

Craft an **Ember Shrine** (a campfire with gold ingots, blaze powder and polished deepslate) and
kindle it. Each shrine takes a name from the land around it.

- **Rest** to restore HP and FP, refill your flasks and bind your respawn.
- **Level Up, Reinforce, Infuse** and divide your **Flasks** between Ember (HP) and Azure (FP).
- **Travel** to any shrine you have kindled.
- Trade at the **Merchant**, exchange **Relics** for legendary armaments, equip a **Crown Shard**, or
  use a Tear of Rebirth to reshape your build.

![Sovereigns](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-sovereigns.png)

![Seven crowns, sundered](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-sovereigns.png)

Combine six **Sovereign Fragments**, carried by champions, with a token to craft a sigil. Use the
sigil to call its Sovereign. Each one has its own abilities, a second phase and a boss bar.

| Sovereign | Level | Token | Crown shard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Dusk Rider | 15 | Clock | — (also rides at night) |
| Varghast the Grafted | 20 | Iron Block | +5 to every attribute |
| Sylvara, Frost Matron | 25 | Blue Ice | +15% max health, immune to frostbite |
| Kaelthar the Stormcaller | 30 | Lightning Rod | +20% lightning damage, +30% stamina recovery |
| Ysolde, the Crimson Bloom | 35 | Redstone Block | Kills restore 3% health, bleed procs 2% |
| Morgrath, Ash Sovereign | 50 | Netherite Scrap | +15% physical damage, immune to fire |
| Vaelorn, the Wyrm-Ravager | 70 | Dragon's Breath | Spells cost 20% less FP, FP recovers twice as fast |

- A felled Sovereign drops a **relic**, which you exchange at a shrine for one of its legendary
  armaments, and its **crown shard**.
- The **Ender Dragon** (crown shard: +10% to all damage dealt), **Wither**, **Warden** and **Elder
  Guardian** drop relics of their own.
- Fair multiplayer rewards: everyone who fought gets runes and loot.

![Better Together](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-ras.png)

![Runeborn and RPG Attribute System](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-ras.png)

Runeborn shares its progression with **RPG Attribute System**.

- Shrine level-ups need RPG Attribute System installed.
- Every rune level you buy at a shrine also grants **RPG levels**, through its own level-up pipeline:
  points, rewards and its banner. Spend those points in its **Level Up** screen (<kbd>K</kbd>).
- The two HUDs stack cleanly: Runeborn's bars move below its level display, wherever you anchor it.
- Without RPG Attribute System, Runeborn still runs. Your origin's attributes carry you while you
  reinforce, infuse and hunt, and the shrine explains how to unlock leveling.
- `rpgLevelsPerRuneLevel` sets how many RPG levels each rune level grants, and
  `levelUpRequiresRpgAttributeSystem: false` lets Runeborn level on its own.

![The Codex](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-codex.png)

![Everything explained](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-codex.png)

- The **Codex** (status screen, <kbd>I</kbd>) has chapters on runes, shrines, attributes, flasks,
  armaments, arts, combat, the world, Sovereigns and controls.
- It shows the **crafting grids** for the shrine, flasks, stones, every whetstone and every sigil.
  The same recipes are added to your recipe book.
- **Feats:** a Runeborn advancement tab with 22 milestones, from kindling your first shrine to
  claiming every crown shard.

![Everything Else](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-everything.png)

![A HUD that reads at a glance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-hud.png)

- HP, FP and stamina bars that grow with your stats, a rune counter, flask charges, your weapon art,
  the target's level and status buildup, and Sovereign boss bars. Vanilla hearts and armor are hidden.
- **Stamina** for attacks and dodges, and a **dodge roll** with brief invulnerability on
  <kbd>Left Alt</kbd>.
- Foes gain **levels** with distance from spawn, and the Nether and the End are deadlier still.
  **Champions** roll affixes such as Ironclad, Frenzied, Stormtouched, Warded or Phasing.
- Cinematic banners for the moments that matter: shrine kindled, champion slain, Sovereign felled,
  runes recovered, and YOU DIED.

![Configuration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-config.png)

![Every number is yours](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/card-config.png)

- `config/runeborn-server.json`: rune gain, level, smithing and merchant cost multipliers, rune loss
  on death, stamina, PvP damage, mob level curves per dimension, champion chance, Dusk Rider spawns,
  shrine travel and the RPG Attribute System link.
- `config/runeborn-client.json`: HUD toggles, scale and offset, bar numbers, banners, target and
  boss bars.
- Operator commands: `/runeborn runes`, `/runeborn attribute`, `/runeborn origin reset`,
  `/runeborn summon`, `/runeborn reload`, `/runeborn info`.

![Questions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/fine_tuned_weaponry/main/marketing/images/header-faq.png)

**Why can't I level up at the shrine?** Install RPG Attribute System alongside Runeborn, or set
`levelUpRequiresRpgAttributeSystem` to `false` in `config/runeborn-server.json`.

**How do I craft the Ember Shrine?** Open the Codex (<kbd>I</kbd>, then **Codex**) or your recipe
book. It is a campfire with gold ingots, blaze powder and polished deepslate.

**Where do I get Sovereign Fragments?** From champions: about one hostile mob in 25 spawns with a gold
title such as "Ironclad Zombie" and carries them (`eliteChance`).

**Does it work with other weapon mods?** Yes. Swords, axes and tridents from other mods get scaling
from your attributes. Turn it off with `vanillaWeaponsScale`.

**Can I use it on a server?** Yes. Player data is saved per world, and every value is
server-authoritative.
